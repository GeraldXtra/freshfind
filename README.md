# FreshFind

Fresh All Along.

Finding a farmers market in Lagos is mostly word of mouth. Someone tells you about a market, you show up and it is closed, or the produce you came for finished before you arrived. The information exists, but it is scattered across flyers, group chats and memory.

FreshFind puts it all in one place. You can see every market near you, the exact days and hours it opens, and the produce that is likely to be fresh that week, so you plan your visit and go once. There is also a produce guide that shows what is in season and which markets sell it, plus a simple assistant you can ask questions like which markets open on Saturday.

The whole site runs in the browser. No accounts, no servers, nothing to install. Just useful market information that loads fast on any device. We built FreshFind for TechWiz 7, the world tech championship by Aptech, under the eGreen Basket theme.
