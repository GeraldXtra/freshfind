# Demo Video Plan

The brief makes a demo video mandatory. It must show every feature of the site working. This is the plan so nothing gets missed on recording day.

## Before recording
- Run npm run build and npm run preview, and record from the built site, not the dev server.
- Use a clean browser window with no extensions and no old session data.
- Record at 1080p with a screen recorder such as OBS or the Windows Game Bar.
- Keep the whole video between six and eight minutes.
- Each member demonstrates the pages they built and speaks for that part. Gerald opens and closes.

## Scenes in order
1. Gerald: Home. Show the logo, headline and search panel. Use Where, When and What to find markets and show the results.
2. Gerald: Home highlights. Show markets near you with open now badges, and in season this week.
3. Amanda: Market Directory. Filter by area, then by day, then by produce type. Switch on open now. Sort by name, by distance after allowing location, and by next open day.
4. Ibrahim: Market Detail. Open a market from a card. Show the breadcrumb, banner, weekly schedule with today highlighted, typical produce grid, map and get directions.
5. Osakue: Produce Guide. Move through the category tabs. Open a produce item and follow a found at link to a market.
6. Osakue: Seasonal Picks. Show this week's top picks and the season calendar.
7. Gerald: Chatbot. Open the floating button, tap a quick reply, then type a question. Show the answer and follow its link to a page.
8. Gerald: Bookmarks. Bookmark a market and a produce item from their cards. Point at the badge count in the navbar. Open the Bookmarks page, add a note, export the list, show the share buttons, remove an item.
9. Amanda: Contact. Show the details and the map with the visitor's location after allowing permission.
10. Ibrahim: About. Show the mission, why FreshFind and the team.
11. Gerald: Cross page features. Open the search overlay from another page, show breadcrumbs, hover effects on cards, the live clock and visitor counter in the footer, and that the login button does nothing by design.
12. Gerald: Responsive. Shrink the window to phone width. Show the menu button, the stacked layout and one page working on mobile.
13. Gerald: Close. Show the Lighthouse scores briefly and end on the home page.

## Checklist before we call it done
Every item in sections 1.6 and 1.7 of the SRS appears on screen at least once. Watch the recording back with the SRS open and tick each one.
